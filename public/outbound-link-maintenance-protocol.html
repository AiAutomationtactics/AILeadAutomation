Check Outgoing Links Without Endorsing Unverified Offers

A repeatable method to test outgoing resources, redirects, affiliate disclosures and link changes without making unsupported claims.

Scope: This is an educational worksheet, not a promise of search ranking, sales or indexing. Examples are illustrative. No paid referral or checkout is required.

Why a working link is not enough

A destination can return HTTP 200 while showing the wrong offer, an expired promotion or an unrelated page. A redirect can change the merchant or strip attribution. Treat technical reachability, factual accuracy and suitability as separate checks. A successful click test never proves a product is safe, fairly priced or appropriate for a particular audience.

Create an inventory with categories

Record the exact URL, category (official documentation, independent research, owner educational page, affiliate referral or payment checkout), intended destination, page where it appears and the date checked. Preserve attribution query strings when testing referrals; do not casually rewrite a merchant's tracking URL. For the owner's Herculist inventory, the Gold3 query is material, while the www and non-www smartsolos hostnames may be variants of one offer rather than separate offers.

Test redirects carefully

Request the URL and inspect the final address, status, page title and visible content. A HEAD request can fail on a healthy site, so confirm with a normal browser or GET where appropriate. Distinguish 404, 403, timeouts, login gates and geo-restrictions. Avoid automated high-volume requests or bypassing anti-bot controls. If you cannot inspect the destination, label it unverified rather than inventing what it sells.

Check claims independently

For a commercial referral, record the product name, who it serves, current price and currency if displayed, recurring charges, refund policy, fulfillment and relevant merchant terms. Check the actual offer page and authoritative policy before describing any of these facts publicly. A six-link Stripe checkout inventory is not six verified products: payment URLs remain in a distinct category until product and fulfillment details are confirmed.

Disclose and label commercial relationships

When a recommendation has an affiliate connection, place a clear disclosure next to the recommendation, not only on a distant policy page. Use rel='sponsored nofollow noopener noreferrer' on promotional external links that open in a new tab. Do not add a referral link merely because it is available in an inventory. If a page is educational, it may be more helpful to link only to neutral documentation or omit commercial links entirely.

Worked example: a changed destination

Suppose a guide links to an official analytics help article and a paid traffic service. The official article redirects to a new documentation path with the same topic; update the link and note the change. The paid service loads but no longer shows the feature the guide describes. Remove the unsupported description and pause its promotional link until verified. Never infer that an old advertised price remains valid.

Copyable review record

Track: source page; link text; original URL; category; HTTP/browser result; final URL; content match; merchant-term check; disclosure proximity; rel attributes; action; checked date; evidence note. Actions can be retain, correct, replace with a primary source, unlink, or defer. Keep a correction log for factual edits; change sitemap lastmod only if actual page content materially changes.

Limits and next steps

Link checking does not establish merchant authorization or Search Console ownership. Do not insert payment buttons into unrelated SEO lessons. For related decision-making, use the sales-claim evidence audit and audience-fit worksheet, subject to live URL verification.

Editorial sources and disclosure

For search guidance, consult Google Search Central's crawlable links guidance and W3C's link-purpose guidance. Commercial referrals: none in this guide. The owner may earn commissions from other clearly disclosed links elsewhere on the site.
