Methodology and limitations

Exact formulas

Leads = sessions × lead rate; sales = leads × sale rate; gross commission = sales × payout; estimated net = gross − campaign costs. Monthly sessions for a target = target ÷ (target months × lead rate × sale rate × payout). Break-even sales = campaign costs ÷ payout, rounded up to a whole sale. Rates are expressed as decimals in the calculations.

Why results might differ

Refunds, delayed payouts, commission eligibility, device and channel mix, attribution gaps, low-quality visits, consent rates, and changing offers all affect real results. Fractions of expected sales are averages across hypothetical repeated campaigns, not actual orders.

How should I monitor a campaign?

For each month, record channel, actual website sessions, consented leads, confirmed purchases, reversed commissions, actual costs and net payouts. Keep forecasts separate from actual results.
